# Helpdesk Copilot — client features

- F1: Cited policy answers — An employee asks a policy question and gets a streamed answer with inline citations that open the exact passage in the source document.
- F2: Open an IT ticket from chat — When the employee's problem needs IT, the assistant drafts a ServiceNow ticket, shows it for confirmation, submits it and returns the ticket number.
- F3: Nightly SharePoint sync — Documents added, changed or removed in a connected SharePoint library are reflected in answers by the next morning without anyone pressing a button.
- F4: Thumbs-down review queue — A thumbs-down with a reason is stored, appears in the knowledge team's review queue, and can be promoted to the eval question set.
- F5: Permission-aware answers — An employee only ever gets answers grounded in documents they are allowed to open in SharePoint.
- F6: Weekly quality report with regression alert — Every week the knowledge team gets a Slack message with answer-quality scores and an alert when a score drops below threshold.
