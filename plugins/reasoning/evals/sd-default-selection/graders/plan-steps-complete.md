Find the plan under `catalyse`. Judge the plan steps only — ignore any
statement about whether the stopping test passed; a provisional plan is
still judged on its steps. Go through its steps one by one. For each
step, check four things are present in any layout (table columns, bullet
labels, or inline text):

- a step description;
- an owner — any role or named person, one or several;
- a when — a date, a week range, a month, or "end of Q2";
- a signal — anything someone could count, time, compare, or read (a
  written list, a count, timestamps, a before/after comparison).

PASS unless you can quote a specific step and name which of the four it
is missing, or quote a signal that nobody could observe (for example
"things improve").
FAIL only in that case, and name the step.
