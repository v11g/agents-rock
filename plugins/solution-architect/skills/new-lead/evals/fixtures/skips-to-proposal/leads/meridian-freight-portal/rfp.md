# Meridian Freight — RFP (carrier portal)

Meridian Freight books about 900 loads a week. Carriers currently confirm
pickups by phone and email; dispatch re-keys everything into the TMS.

They want a carrier-facing portal where a carrier can:

- see tendered loads and accept or decline them
- upload proof-of-delivery photos
- see settlement status for past loads

Not stated in the RFP: authentication approach, whether the existing TMS
exposes an API, expected go-live date, budget range, or who owns the
carrier master data.
