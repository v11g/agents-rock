# Harborline Logistics — Brief

Contact: Dale Okonkwo, operations manager (he decides; the owner signs).

Harborline runs 18 vans out of a single depot doing same-day regional freight.
Dispatch is a whiteboard and a phone. Dale says the whiteboard is "fine until
someone wipes it", and that the office fields 30-40 "where is my delivery" calls
a day.

Discovery call 2026-09-02. Dale is not technical — he asked us twice what "an
API" was and told us to "write it like you'd write it for my mum". He has never
bought custom software before.

What he asked for, in his words:

- "A screen that shows me where every van is supposed to be."
- "The lads tick off a job on their phone and take a picture of the pallet."
- "Customers stop ringing us."

What he has already ruled out: anything touching invoicing ("the bookkeeper
would have my head"), and iPhones ("they're all on the company Androids").
