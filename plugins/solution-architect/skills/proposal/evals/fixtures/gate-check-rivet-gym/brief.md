# Rivet Gym — Brief

Contact: Marco Benitez, owner (decides alone).

Three sites, about 900 members. Class booking lives in a shared spreadsheet and
a WhatsApp group; two staff spend Monday chasing lapsed memberships by phone.

Discovery call 2026-09-11. Marco is not technical. He has had two quotes from
other shops already and says both were "a number on a page with nothing behind
it".

What he asked for:

- "Members book their own classes, and the class stops taking bookings when it
  is full."
- "A screen on the door that tells the lad on reception if you are paid up."
- "I want to stop paying two people to ring people."

What is decided: card payments stay where they are. What is not decided: whether
the three sites share one class schedule or run their own.

No effort estimate has been done for this lead yet — the architecture call
happened, the estimating session did not.
