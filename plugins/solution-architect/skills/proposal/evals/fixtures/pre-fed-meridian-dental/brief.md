# Meridian Dental Group — Brief

Contact: Dr. Alina Vogt, managing partner (she and two other partners sign off
together; there is no IT manager).

Four chairs, two receptionists, roughly 3,100 active patients. Reception spends
most of Monday morning on the paper recall list and fielding "what did I have
done and what do I owe" calls.

Discovery call 2026-09-08. Alina is comfortable with everyday software words —
website, app, database, login — but not infrastructure. She runs the practice
management system herself and exports from it weekly.

What she said, in her words:

- "Patients should be able to look up their own history without ringing us."
- "The six-month recall has to send itself."
- "It is patient health data. I need to be able to show who looked at what."

Money: the partners agreed a ceiling before the call. Alina's line was "if it
comes in over that, cut the scope, do not come back with a bigger number."

Already ruled out: insurance claims (phase 2), online booking (they like the
phone), and anything that writes back into the practice management system.
